PC Backup Sync

A small personal Windows app that backs up folders from my computer to my own Google Drive.

What it does

  • Uploads new and changed files from folders I choose to a "PC Backup" folder in my Google Drive.
  • Runs on a schedule (for example every hour) while the computer is on.
  • Never deletes anything, on the computer or on Google Drive.

Google data it uses

The app asks for the drive.file permission, which only lets it see and change the files it creates itself. It cannot read anything else in your Google Drive. It also reads your email address to show which account is connected.
