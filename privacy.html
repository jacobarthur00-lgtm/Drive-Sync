Privacy Policy

PC Backup Sync · Last updated November 23, 2026

PC Backup Sync is a personal Windows app that copies files from folders you choose on your computer to your own Google Drive. This policy explains what data it uses.

Data the app accesses

  • Files in the folders you select on your computer. They are read and uploaded to your Google Drive. The app never changes or deletes them.
  • Google Drive files the app created (Google's drive.file permission). The app cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Your Google account email address, only to show which account is connected.

Where data goes

Your files go directly from your computer to your Google Drive through Google's API. There is no other server. The app's developer does not receive, collect, store or see your files, your email address or any other data.

Data stored on your computer

The app keeps its settings, a list of files already backed up, and its Google sign-in on your computer, in your Windows user profile. The sign-in is encrypted with Windows' built-in protection so only your Windows account can read it.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

You can disconnect the app in its settings, or remove its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling means deleting the app and its settings folder (%APPDATA%\DriveFolderSync). Files already backed up stay in your Google Drive until you delete them.
